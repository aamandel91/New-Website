## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your browser or device when you visit. Cookies let a site recognize your device on return visits, remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. Some cookies are set by the site you are visiting ("first-party cookies") and others are set by third parties whose services we embed in the Site ("third-party cookies").

This policy uses "cookies" as a shorthand for cookies and similar technologies, which include browser local storage, session storage, pixels (also called web beacons or tracking pixels), and software development kits (SDKs) used by embedded third-party services. They work in slightly different ways but serve the same general purposes described below.

## How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

- Keep you signed in to your account and remember the actions you take during a session, such as the search filters you have applied or the listings you have saved.
- Remember whether you have already seen and dismissed our cookie-consent notice, so we do not show it to you on every page.
- Measure how visitors use the Site so we can understand which pages, searches, and listings are useful and where the Site can be improved.
- Operate the maps, listing data, and accessibility tools that are part of the Site.
- Help us and our service providers reach prospective home buyers and sellers with relevant information about properties and services.

We do not sell the information collected through cookies. The way we share information generally is described in the <a href={`${tenant.brand.siteUrl}/privacy-policy/`}>Privacy Policy</a>.

## Types of cookies we use

### Strictly necessary and functional cookies

These cookies are required for the Site to work. They cannot be turned off through our cookie settings because the Site will not function correctly without them. They include:

- **Authentication and session cookies** that keep you signed in to a saved-search or favorites account and protect that account from unauthorized use.
- **Consent-state cookies** that remember whether you have acknowledged our cookie banner, so we do not display it again on every page.
- **Preference cookies** that remember the search filters, map area, sort order, and similar choices you make as you browse listings, so the Site behaves consistently as you move from page to page.

### Analytics cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand, in aggregate, how visitors find and use the Site. The reports we receive tell us things like which pages are most viewed, how long visitors spend on a listing page, what search terms are common, and where errors occur. We use this information to fix problems and improve the Site.

The Site uses **Google Analytics** and **Google Tag Manager**, products provided by Google LLC. Google may set cookies in your browser to measure your interaction with the Site. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites</a>, and you can opt out of Google Analytics specifically by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on at <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout</a>.

### Marketing cookies

The Site loads the **SureSend tracking pixel** (identifier SS-UGWGMAAAPH), which is provided by a third-party marketing partner. ## How to manage and disable cookies

You have several ways to control cookies and similar technologies:

- **Browser settings.** Every major browser lets you see what cookies are set, block cookies from specific sites or in general, and delete cookies that have already been set. Look for "Privacy" or "Cookies" in your browser settings, or follow the instructions for your browser:
  - Chrome: <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647</a>
  - Safari: <a href="https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac</a>
  - Firefox: <a href="https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer</a>
  - Edge: <a href="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09</a>

  Blocking cookies will not prevent you from using the Site, but some features — staying signed in, remembering your search filters, the cookie banner not reappearing — depend on cookies and may not work as expected.

- **Industry opt-outs.** You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many companies through the Network Advertising Initiative at <a href="https://optout.networkadvertising.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">optout.networkadvertising.org</a> and the Digital Advertising Alliance at <a href="https://optout.aboutads.info/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">optout.aboutads.info</a>. These opt-outs are set as cookies in your browser, so you will need to apply them in each browser and on each device you use.

- **Mobile devices.** On a mobile device, you can usually limit ad tracking through your device's privacy or advertising settings (for example, "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Reset advertising ID" on Android).

- **Your account and broader privacy choices.** If you have submitted information through the Site or have an account with us, you can ask us to update, correct, or delete that information by following the instructions in our <a href={`${tenant.brand.siteUrl}/privacy-policy/`}>Privacy Policy</a>.

If you want to limit what is collected about your visits to the Site, please use the controls described in the section above.
